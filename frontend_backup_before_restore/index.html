Battery User Guide

Learn about your 12V, 7Ah lead-acid battery.

1. Understanding Your 12V, 7Ah Battery

Your battery is a 12V, 7Ah lead-acid battery. Its nominal energy is approximately 84Wh (12V × 7Ah). Actual usable energy depends on battery condition, load, temperature and discharge rate.

2. What Is State of Charge (SOC)?

SOC estimates how much charge remains in the battery. It is expressed as a percentage. A high SOC indicates more remaining charge. A low SOC indicates that the battery may need charging.

3. What Is State of Health (SOH)?

SOH estimates the battery's condition compared with its original condition. A reliable SOH estimate requires suitable battery measurements and an estimation method. It should not be presented as measured data if the device does not provide it.

4. Understanding Voltage and Current

Voltage describes the electrical potential of the battery. Current describes the rate of electrical flow. The sign of current depends on how the sensor is connected and configured.

5. Understanding Battery Temperature

Temperature monitoring helps identify overheating conditions. The temperature sensor's location matters: an ambient temperature reading may differ from the actual battery temperature.

6. Demo Mode vs Live Mode

Demo Mode: Displays sample readings for exploring the dashboard. These are not measurements from your physical battery.

Live Mode: Displays readings received from your ESP32. If the device is disconnected or has not sent data, the dashboard should show that live readings are unavailable rather than inventing values.

7. Battery Safety Reminders

Use a charger designed for your battery type. Avoid short circuits, incorrect polarity, overheating and physical damage. Follow the battery manufacturer's charging and handling instructions.
